# Flashcards

> Spaced repetition study cards powered by the [Spaced Repetition](https://github.com/st3v3nmw/obsidian-spaced-repetition) plugin.

## Format

Each card uses the following format, with `?` on its own line separating question from answer:

```
Question text
?
Answer text
```

Separate cards with a blank line. You can ask the LLM to generate cards from any wiki page:

> "Please generate 10 spaced repetition flashcards from [[some-page]] and add them to the appropriate section in this file."

---

## Example Cards

What are the three core operations of LLM Wiki?
?
Ingest, Query, and Lint

What is the key difference between LLM Wiki and a RAG system?
?
RAG extracts information from raw documents at query time — knowledge does not accumulate. LLM Wiki actively integrates knowledge into wiki pages at ingest time, so the knowledge base continuously grows.
